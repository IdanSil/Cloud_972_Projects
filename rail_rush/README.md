# Rail Rush

An endless subway runner you control with your body through the webcam.

- Step left / right: switch tracks
- Jump: hop over red barriers (reacts to upward speed, not just height)
- Duck: roll under yellow signs and plasma shots
- Raise one hand above your head: fire your blaster at drones
- Raise both hands: start / play again

Power-ups: magnet, jetpack, super jump, 2× score, shield, turbo smash.
Bamboozles (mystery boxes and traps hidden in coin trails): mirror controls, blackout, dizzy camera, hyper speed, moon gravity.
Enemies: laser drones that block a track, slider drones that move between tracks, gunner drones that fire plasma, and oncoming trains.

Open `index.html` from any https:// address (camera access needs https). It also plays with keys (arrows + F to fire) or swipes and taps.

Built with three.js (3D) and MediaPipe Pose (body tracking, runs on your device).
