Tips for better body tracking
  • Put the camera at about waist height, pointing straight at you.
  • Stand 2–3 m back so your whole body fits, feet included. Visible feet make jumps more reliable.
  • Light the room from the front. A bright window behind you makes tracking worse.
  • Keep other people out of the picture.
  • On a Mac you can use your iPhone as the webcam (Continuity Camera). It has a sharper, wider lens.
